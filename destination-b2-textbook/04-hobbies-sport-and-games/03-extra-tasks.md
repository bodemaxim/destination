# Extra tasks

## 1. Multiple choice

Choose the phrasal verb that fits.

<QuizBlock unit="Unit 4 · Hobbies, sport and games" exercise="1. Multiple choice">

<MultipleChoice
  question="They had to ___ the final because the weather was going to get worse."
  :options="['bring forward', 'put up with', 'take to']"
  answer="bring forward"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="Please ___ playing; we still have ten minutes left on the clock."

/>
