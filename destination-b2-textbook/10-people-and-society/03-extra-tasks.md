# Extra tasks

Practice the Unit 10 phrasal verbs. Check each item as you go. Spelling is not case-sensitive.

## 1. Multiple choice

Choose the phrasal verb that fits.

<QuizBlock unit="Unit 10 · People and society" exercise="1. Multiple choice">

<MultipleChoice
  question="Tell your aunt I ___ her when you see her at the weekend."
  :options="['asked after', 'looked down on', 'picked on']"
  answer="asked after"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="She ___ three children on her own after her husband left."
  :options="['brought up', 'passed away', 'took aback']"
  answer="brought up"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="They ___ over money and did not speak to each other for a year."
  :options="['fell out', 'made up', 'grew up']"
  answer="fell out"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="I ___ with my flatmates; we cook together almost every evening."
  :options="['get on', 'look up to', 'put down']"
  answer="get on"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="He ___ in a small village and still goes back there every summer."
  :options="['grew up', 'settled down', 'fell for']"
  answer="grew up"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="Some students ___ anyone who does not wear expensive clothes."
  :options="['look down on', 'stand up for', 'ask after']"
  answer="look down on"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="I have always ___ my grandfather because he treats everyone with respect."
  :options="['looked up to', 'picked on', 'fallen out']"
  answer="looked up to"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="The older boys used to ___ him in the playground until a teacher stopped them."
  :options="['pick on', 'bring up', 'make up']"

<MultipleChoice
  question="Do not ___ her in front of the group; it only makes her more nervous."
