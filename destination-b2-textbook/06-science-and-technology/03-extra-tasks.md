# Extra tasks

Practice the Unit 6 phrasal verbs. Check each item as you go. Spelling is not case-sensitive.

## 1. Multiple choice

Choose the phrasal verb that fits.

<QuizBlock unit="Unit 6 · Science and technology" exercise="1. Multiple choice">

<MultipleChoice
  question="The laboratory freezer ___ overnight, and all the samples were ruined."
  :options="['broke down', 'came off', 'plugged in']"
  answer="broke down"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="The students will ___ the same experiment three times to check the results."
  :options="['turn off', 'carry out', 'cut off']"
  answer="carry out"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="If this plan ___, we will have a cheaper way to store solar energy."
  :options="['comes off', 'gives off', 'narrows down']"
  answer="comes off"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="Nobody could ___ a name for the new chemical that they had produced."
  :options="['put through', 'come up with', 'turn into']"
  answer="come up with"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="The storm ___ electricity to half the city for almost six hours."
  :options="['cut off', 'found out', 'worked out']"
  answer="cut off"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="We need to ___ why the robot keeps stopping after two minutes."
  :options="['find out', 'plug in', 'give off']"
  answer="find out"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="You have to ___ the charger before the laptop will start charging."
  :options="['come on', 'plug in', 'break down']"
  answer="plug in"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="Could you ___ to the research department, please?"
  :options="['put me through', 'turn me off', 'carry me out']"
  answer="put me through"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="Please ___ the microscope when you have finished looking at the slides."
  :options="['turn off', 'come off', 'find out']"
  answer="turn off"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="It took us an hour to ___ the answer to the last physics problem."
  :options="['work out', 'cut off', 'come on']"
  answer="work out"
/>

</QuizBlock>
