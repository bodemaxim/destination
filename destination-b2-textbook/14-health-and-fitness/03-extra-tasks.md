# Extra tasks

Practice the Unit 14 phrasal verbs. Check each item as you go. Spelling is not case-sensitive.

## 1. Multiple choice

Choose the phrasal verb that fits.

<QuizBlock unit="Unit 14 · Health and fitness" exercise="1. Multiple choice">

<MultipleChoice
  question="A fire ___ in the hospital kitchen, and two wards had to be emptied."
  :options="['broke out', 'wore off', 'put on']"
  answer="broke out"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="Cold weather often ___ her asthma, so she stays inside on icy mornings."
  :options="['brings on', 'puts down', 'comes to']"
  answer="brings on"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="Three players ___ a stomach bug the night before the match."
  :options="['came down with', 'pulled through', 'gave up']"
  answer="came down with"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="She ___ in the ambulance and asked what had happened in the gym."
  :options="['came round', 'passed out', 'cut down']"
  answer="came round"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="The doctor told him to ___ smoking if he wanted his cough to improve."
  :options="['cut down', 'look after', 'put down']"
  answer="cut down"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="I do not ___ a long run today; my throat still hurts when I swallow."

/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="It took her a month to ___ the flu and go back to swimming."

<MultipleChoice
