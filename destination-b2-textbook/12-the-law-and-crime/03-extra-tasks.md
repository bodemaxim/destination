## 1. Multiple choice

Choose the phrasal verb that fits.

<QuizBlock unit="Unit 12 · The law and crime" exercise="1. Multiple choice">

<MultipleChoice
  question="The minister would not ___ after the protest, and the new camera law stayed."
  :options="['back down', 'break out', 'make off']"
  answer="back down"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="Three prisoners ___ last night and are still missing."
  :options="['broke out', 'came forward', 'handed in']"
  answer="broke out"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="The government will ___ a new law against selling knives to teenagers."
  :options="['bring in', 'take in', 'let off']"
  answer="bring in"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="Two officers ___ the motorbike through the market, but they lost it."
  :options="['chased after', 'looked into', 'backed down']"

/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="Police asked anyone who saw the robbery to ___ with information."

/>

<MultipleChoice
