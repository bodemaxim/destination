# Extra tasks

Practice the Unit 8 phrasal verbs. Check each item as you go. Spelling is not case-sensitive.

## 1. Multiple choice

Choose the phrasal verb that fits.

<QuizBlock unit="Unit 8 · The media" exercise="1. Multiple choice">

<MultipleChoice
  question="I did not want to ___ politics at dinner, but someone mentioned the election."
  :options="['bring up', 'hand out', 'come on']"
  answer="bring up"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="The documentary ___ at nine, so we have to finish dinner before then."
  :options="['comes on', 'fills in', 'stands out']"
  answer="comes on"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="Her first novel ___ last spring and was reviewed in every Sunday paper."
  :options="['came out', 'turned over', 'looked up']"
  answer="came out"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="Please ___ this form before we can send you the press pass."

  answer="fill in"
/>

<MultipleChoice

<MultipleChoice
