# Extra tasks

Practice the Unit 2 phrasal verbs. Check each item as you go. Spelling is not case-sensitive.

## 1. Multiple choice

Choose the phrasal verb that fits.

<QuizBlock unit="Unit 2 · Travel and transport" exercise="1. Multiple choice">

<MultipleChoice
  question="We had to ___ at the airport three hours before our flight to Tokyo."
  :options="['check in', 'take off', 'pull in']"
  answer="check in"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="Could you ___ at the station on your way into town?"
  :options="['run over', 'drop me off', 'see me off']"
  answer="drop me off"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="The plane ___ twenty minutes late because of thick fog over the runway."
  :options="['checked out', 'set out', 'took off']"
  answer="took off"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="We went to the station to ___ before she left for university."
  :options="['see her off', 'pick her up', 'catch up with her']"
  answer="see her off"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="They ___ for the airport far too late and nearly missed their flight."
  :options="['checked in', 'set off', 'turned round']"
  answer="set off"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="We need to ___ of the hotel by eleven if we want the last ferry."
  :options="['check out', 'drop off', 'get back']"
  answer="check out"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="We did not ___ from the airport until well after midnight last night."
  :options="['take off', 'get back', 'go away']"
  answer="get back"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="It took us an hour to ___ the other cyclists on the mountain road."
  :options="['catch up with', 'run over', 'make for']"
  answer="catch up with"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="A careless driver nearly ___ a cyclist who was crossing the busy junction."
  :options="['picked up', 'ran over', 'dropped off']"
  answer="ran over"
/>

<MultipleChoice
  question="It was hard to ___ the tour group as they rushed through the museum."
  :options="['keep up with', 'see off', 'check in']"
  answer="keep up with"
/>

</QuizBlock>
